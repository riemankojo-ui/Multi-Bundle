Multi Bundle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Multi Bundle (multibundle.vercel.app) is a web and Android app that lets you import website projects, edit and preview them, and export them as bundles. It is made by wunateh Kojo.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use only this basic profile information. We do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

If you use the cloud feature, the text of your projects (code and file names) is stored so it can sync between your devices.

If you choose to connect GitHub, the app accesses only the repositories you pick, to import or publish your project files.

How we use it

Your information is used only to sign you in, show your account, and save and sync your projects. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for ads.

Where it is stored

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication and project text is stored in Firebase Firestore, both services of Google. The app is hosted on Vercel.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To have your account data and stored projects deleted, email us at the address below and we will remove them.

Children

Multi Bundle is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
