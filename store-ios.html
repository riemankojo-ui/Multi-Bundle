Apple needs a Developer account ($99/year). Without signing secrets this makes an unsigned archive only, which can't be installed or uploaded. For a real .ipa, add these repo secrets (Settings, Secrets and variables, Actions): IOS_CERT_P12_B64, IOS_CERT_PASSWORD, IOS_PROFILE_B64, IOS_TEAM_ID. This iOS workflow is new and untested, so expect to fix a first-run error.
