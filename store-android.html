APP DETAILS

Create gives you 4 files: app.aab, signing.keystore, assetlinks.json and readme.html. The signing key is made on the first run and kept in a private draft release in your repo; later runs reuse it. Do not delete that draft. The AAB, assetlinks and readme are public if your repo is public; the keystore and its password are not.
